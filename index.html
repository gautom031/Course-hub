Course Description

Design I introduces the foundational principles of visual communication, formal composition, and creative problem-solving workflows. Students will explore the interplay of shape, line, typography, color theory, and spatial organization through a series of structured studio projects and collaborative critiques.

The curriculum emphasizes critical thinking, iterative experimentation, and practical execution using contemporary design instruments. By the conclusion of this term, participants will have established a comprehensive portfolio demonstrating individual stylistic voice, technical proficiency, and rigorous formal design strategies.
